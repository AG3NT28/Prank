New Artificial Intelligence Course Announced for Students

29 September 2026   |   Academic Affairs

Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras) has launched five courses on Artificial Intelligence through SWAYAM Plus that are being offered free of cost. These 25 to 45 hours courses, being professionals.

The course provides students with an introduction to modern AI concepts, machine learning fundamentals, real-world applications and emerging technologies. The key focus of these courses is to make AI available for every one as no prior AI knowledge required to apply for these courses. Those interested can register through the following link with 2nd October 2026 being the last date to register -

About the Course

The programme has been designed for students who are interested in developing their understanding of artificial intelligence and exploring its applications across different industries.

Course Information

Course Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
Duration 6 Weeks
Mode Online
Eligibility Open to Students
Course Fee Free
Certificate Available on Completion
Registration is open to eligible students.
